**MARKET MAYHEM 📈💰**

Think you have what it takes to become a stock market millionaire?

MARKET MAYHEM is an interactive stock market trading simulation where your goal is to turn **$10,000 into $20,000 in just 30 in-game days**.

Buy stocks, sell at the right moment, follow breaking fake financial news, and react to unpredictable market movements. Every decision matters, and one bad investment could cost you big!

**🎮 Features**
- 📊 Dynamic stock market with constantly changing prices.
- ⏱️ Real-time simulation: every 90 seconds equals one in-game day.
- 📰 Random breaking news that can influence stock prices.
- 💼 Portfolio tracking and trading history.
- 🔍 Company research and detailed reports.
- 🎁 Investor Hub with daily challenges, mystery crates, and a prize wheel.
- 🏆 Achievements and rewards.
- 🎯 A challenging goal: reach $20,000 before your 30-day deadline.

**Can you beat the market, build your fortune, and become the ultimate investor?**

Welcome to MARKET MAYHEM. The market never sleeps!
